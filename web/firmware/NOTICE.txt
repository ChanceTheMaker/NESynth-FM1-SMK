NESynth experimental FM-1 and SMK-37 firmware

fm1-template.fwsc contains only an original diagnostic generated by
tools/diagnostic.py. User cartridges are inserted in the browser and are not
uploaded. NESynth integration is GPL-3.0-only; InfoNES is Apache-2.0.
See the repository LICENSE, vendor/infones, and vendor/felucca notices.

The firmware packaging, USB transport, installer, restore and progress modules
are adapted from ../arcade-fm1-smk (GameDude), retaining the Felucca platform.
Complete build sources and patches are in this repository. The browser template
is generated by tools/public_template.py; template.json records its hashes.

fm1-original.fwsc is the unmodified M-VAVE FM-1 V15 restore package.
Its origin and SHA-256 are recorded in original.json. It is separate from
NESynth's open-source firmware and retains its original vendor terms.

FM-1 hardware: picture, controls, both cores and update/restart exercised.
Full-speed emulation and smooth audio remain under development.
SMK-37 Pro: owner-build installation, playback and restart exercised.
Public calibration-free core: packaging, browser flows and protected flash
simulations pass; this exact binary and Elite hardware remain unvalidated.

smk37-template.bin is a raw diagnostic-only application, not an installable
package. tools/public_smk_template.py generates it; smk37-template.json records
its hashes. The browser builds the model-specific package locally from a pinned,
hash-verified official M-VAVE 016 file. Vendor files retain their original terms.
The SMK RAM updater and browser packaging are adapted from Doom/Felucca/Sloop;
source hashes, credits and GPL notices are retained in vendor/smk-updater.

nesynth-fm1-969.bin and nesynth-smk37-969.bin are the NESynth library cores.
nesynth.json pins their exact bytes; the browser packages them automatically
with the verified model-specific official base. They contain no game library
or owner calibration. Bluetooth adaptations retain the GPL-3.0-only Hortator
source at 7e3415971c5800b4a3ad0146a7d95615883d7d96 and its pinned JieLi SDK
components and notices. Build inputs and adaptations are recorded in
tools/build_bluetooth_probe.py and experiments/bluetooth/.
